dulaimi89 ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application "spaceball" (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you play our game.

## 1. Information We Collect Automatically
Our App does not require you to create an account or provide personal contact information (like your name or email). However, to support gameplay via advertisements, our third-party advertising partner automatically collects certain non-personal device information.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)
This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process data about your device to serve relevant, personalized, or non-personalized ads. 

The data collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may include:
* **Device Identifiers:** Your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID).
* **IP Address:** Used to estimate your general geographic location (country or city).
* **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with ads (clicks, views, and app launch times).
* **Technical Information:** Device model, operating system version, and mobile network information.

This data is used to deliver ads, limit how many times you see the same ad, combat fraud, and measure ad performance.

To learn more about how Google uses your data, please review the [Google Partner Privacy Policy](https://google.com).

## 3. Children's Privacy
This App is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). When the App is used by a child under the age of 13, the Google AdMob SDK is configured to only request non-personalized ads, and we do not track or collect behavioral advertising data from these users.

## 4. Your Choices and Opt-Out Rights
You can manage or opt-out of personalized advertising through your mobile device settings:
* On Android devices, open the **Settings** app, navigate to **Privacy** > **Ads**, and select **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.
